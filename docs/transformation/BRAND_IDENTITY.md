# ArogyaRakshak — Brand Identity Specification

**Name:** ArogyaRakshak (**आरोग्यरक्षक**) — *Healthcare Protector*  
**Positioning:** Evidence-first healthcare intelligence and patient financial protection for India.  
**Visual Archetype:** *The Sovereign Botanical Shield* — clinical precision meets Indian botanical warmth and legal-archival clarity.

---

## 1. Brand Personality & Voice

| Dimension | ArogyaRakshak IS | ArogyaRakshak IS NOT |
|---|---|---|
| **Tone** | Calm, transparent, protective, plain-spoken, legally precise | Alarmist, sensational ("You were scammed by 1,335%!"), corporate-sterile |
| **Authority** | Earned through visible provenance, cited rules, and named human clinicians | Manufactured through green checkmarks, fake doctor personas, or opaque "AI scores" |
| **Aesthetic** | Warm Ivory parchment, Deep Forest ink, Muted Sage botanical geometry, crisp archival lines | Cyberpunk dark-mode neon, glowing cyan gradients, generic hospital blue/white stock clip-art |
| **Honesty** | Explicitly states *"Not assessed"*, *"Cannot compare reliably"*, and *"Held back for human review"* | Silently guesses, fabricates approval odds, or hides reference-data boundaries |

---

## 2. Visual Signature Elements

1. **The Deep Forest / Warm Ivory Canvas:** Instantly distinguishes ArogyaRakshak from every generic blue healthcare portal in India. Warm Ivory (`#F4F1E8`) feels like dignified archival paper; Deep Forest (`#142F2B`) anchors trust and protection.
2. **The Protective Botanical-Medical Shield Emblem:** An original geometric mark uniting a protective shield contour, subtle botanical leaf symmetry (healing & organic vitality), and an architectural medical cross formed by negative space and fine line geometry.
3. **The Six-Tier Provenance Language:** Colour + icon + explicit text pills (`Verified`, `AI-Derived`, `Human-Reviewed`, `Needs Attention`, `Critical`, `Not Assessed`) act as a signature visual thread across landing pages, module workspaces, PDFs, and the Evidence Explorer.
4. **Bilingual Heritage Accent:** Subtle Devanagari pairing (`आरोग्यरक्षक`) alongside the English Manrope wordmark honors India's linguistic diversity without cluttering navigation.
